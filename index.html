Aleksa Milojević

Aleksa Milojević (born in Vienna, Austria) is a New York City-based urban strategist, designer, researcher, and media producer.

Milojević studied at TU Wien, AHO in Oslo, and the University of Applied Arts Vienna before earning master’s degrees in architecture from Tongji University and Yale University. His work has been exhibited and screened at the Guggenheim Museum Bilbao, Autostadt Wolfsburg, the Yale School of Architecture, and the Yale Center for Collaborative Arts and Media, among others.

His professional background includes independent practice and collaborations with set designers, urban tech startups, and architecture offices in Europe and the United States.

Milojević is part of the design and urban planning collaborative Milojević + Wilde and serves on the board of directors of VOGELFREI Media & Information, a New York–based nonprofit dedicated to urban research.
