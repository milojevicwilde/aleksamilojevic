Aleksa Milojević

Aleksa Milojević (born in Vienna, Austria) is a New York City-based urban strategist, designer, researcher, and media producer. His work spans spatial planning, urban design, and audiovisual media, and addresses contemporary urban conditions, spatial symbolism, and sociocultural participation.

Milojević studied at TU Wien, AHO in Oslo, and the University of Applied Arts Vienna under Kazuyo Sejima. He holds master’s degrees from Tongji University and the Yale School of Architecture, where he studied under Peter Eisenman and Frank Gehry. His work has been exhibited and screened at the Guggenheim Museum Bilbao, Autostadt Wolfsburg, the Yale School of Architecture, and the Yale Center for Collaborative Arts and Media, among others.

His professional background includes independent practice and collaborations with set designers, urban tech startups, and architecture offices in Europe and the United States, among them Wolfgang Tschapeller ZT in Vienna, MITarh in Belgrade, and Hart Howerton in New York City.

Milojević is part of the design and urban planning collaborative Milojević + Wilde and serves on the board of directors of VOGELFREI Media & Information, a New York–based nonprofit dedicated to urban research.
